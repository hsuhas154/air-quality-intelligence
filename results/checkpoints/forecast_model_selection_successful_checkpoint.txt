================================================================================
7. DATA / METHODOLOGY NOTES
================================================================================

Current AQI implementation:
    CPCB-style hourly approximation.

Important limitation:
    CPCB pollutant averaging periods are not yet fully represented in the
    current hourly feature pipeline.

    In particular, proper averaging periods for pollutants such as PM2.5,
    PM10, CO and O3 require further methodological hardening before claiming
    a production-grade CPCB AQI implementation.

Station aggregation:
    Current city-level pollutant aggregation uses the available station
    measurements with coverage gating.

Coverage safeguard:
    PM2.5 station-count thresholds are used to prevent severely under-covered
    hours from generating misleading AQI values.

Missing feature values:
    Training-only median imputation is used within model preprocessing.
    Test data is never used to calculate training imputation statistics.
